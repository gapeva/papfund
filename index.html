The Market Gap & Our Solution

Bridging the critical 12-month limbo between graduation and first salary.

01

The Post-Graduation Cash Cliff

Newly qualified nurses and doctors in Kenya face severe financial distress while waiting for Ministry of Health internship postings or licensing exams (NCK, KMPDC). Without rent, food, or transport money, brilliant professionals stall at the starting line.

  • Traditional banks reject unemployed graduates
  • High licensing and relocation barriers
02

The Pap Fund Bridge Model

We disburse KES 200,000 in monthly tranches for living upkeep and licensing. Repayments begin post-employment at KES 4,200/month over 12 years. 100% of cash is instantly recycled into new graduates.

  • Automated M-Pesa collection & CRB vetting
  • Dual guarantor binding & license verification
Defensibility

Why Medics & Nurses Are the Ultimate Asset Class

Unmatched structural protection compared to general retail and digital credit apps.

Inelastic Career Demand

Healthcare is recession-proof. Hospitals and county governments always require medical staff, ensuring near-100% long-term employment stability.

Global Migration Upside

Kenyan nurses and doctors are heavily recruited by the UK NHS, Germany, US, and Gulf states—multiplying their income and repayment capacity.

Professional Leverage

Tying credit profiles to professional council standing (NCK, KMPDC) provides unmatched social and regulatory enforcement against default.
